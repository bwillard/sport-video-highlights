Keys & help:
Playback
  • Space play / pause · M sound on / off · [ / ] speed −/+ 0.5×
  • ← / → back / forward 5 s (Shift: 60 s) · , / . one frame back / forward
  • Click or drag the timeline to jump; hover it to see the time
  • Z magnifier on / off: a close-up that follows the mouse over the video · Shift+Z 3× / 6×
Clips
  • I / O mark in / out · Enter save the clip (without an in point: the last 8 s and next 2 s) · Esc clear
  • ↑ / ↓ previous / next clip · R replay it · Shift+I / O move its start / end to here
  • 1–9 tags · + / - 😊 / 🙁 · N note
  • F default player · P add a player number · T team play
  • E export · Del delete
Game time
  • H / Shift+H 1st / 2nd half kickoff (H late in the game asks which)
  • J / Shift+J end of the 1st / 2nd half
  • Non-game time is shaded on the timeline. The Game time box (right) suggests the times, jumps to each, and cuts the video down to game time
Callouts
  • C pause inside the selected clip, press C, click on the player (drag to size the ring): the reel freezes there with a spotlight
  • Shift+C remove it
Ball
  • B show the game ball where it has been tracked (solid ring: seen; dashed: filled in across a short gap)
  • K then click the ball = it's here · Shift+K = that ring is wrong, no ball to see. The path is redone around the fix, and (unless switched off next to the ⚽ button) the frame becomes a labelled frame: most go to training, one 30-second moment in five to the benchmark
  • K O other balls: click each spare / next-field ball (cyan), click again to remove, O / Esc to stop (for training; a click on a detection takes its exact spot and size)
  • D (or 🔍 layers) debug layer: every ball detection as a circle with its confidence (dim below 0.25); a grey circle with no ring = seen but dropped by the filter, nothing = never detected; magenta dot = a ball you placed; red × = a detection your fix suppressed; orange "fixed" = on something that never moves all game (a flag, chair, bag: never the ball); dashed box = the area the detector searches
Field map
  • The field map below the video shows everyone the camera sees, from above (🔍 layers turns it on / off): our players as their number where it's known (fainter = guessed: by appearance, at the "auto-label at" setting, or followed on the field from where they're known), a white dot where not; opponents as blue dots. It needs the game's field calibrated once: a few clicks tie the video to the field
  • Q (or ✏️ calibrate) calibrate / correct: a magenta banner over the video says it's on, and clicks on the video place points instead of pausing. Click a spot on the video that you can place on the field (a corner, a goal post, the centre spot, where the circle meets the halfway line, or anywhere on a line or the circle), then click that spot on the map (magenta dots = landmarks; near a line = somewhere on that line). It solves again each time in the background (a few seconds, up to ~30 s on a long game); keep clicking meanwhile, and the clicks made during a solve go into the next one together. Q / Esc stops · U takes back the last click · click one of your clicks (cyan ×) on the video to remove it
  • To start: 4 landmarks not all on one line, or at kickoff the centre spot, both places the circle crosses the halfway line and 3 more points on the circle. Then click goal posts or corners at both ends: they tell it how long and wide the field is
  • Shift+Q (or 📐 field lines) field lines: the calibrated field drawn over the video (magenta), just to look; it's always shown while calibrating. Where it misses the painted lines, add a click there. A red × is a click that disagrees with the rest (often on the other set of lines on a double-marked field): click it to remove it
Analytics
  • Below the field map, once the game's field is calibrated and both halves' times are set. Possession: of the time the ball is in play, how much each team has it, and how much is nobody's: contested (players of both teams within 2 m of the ball for half a second or more), loose (the ball seen with nobody within 3 m, or in the air, for a second or more: a long ball, a clearance, a ball rolling into space), or not known (the ball not seen for over 6 s). The share on the right is ours of the time either team has it. Hover a bar for minutes
  • The timeline: the whole game left to right (1H, then 2H), and where the ball is up the field (up = toward the goal we attack), coloured by who has it; the strip along the bottom is who has it even where the ball isn't seen. Hover for the moment, click to jump there. Goals come from your clips tagged goal: rate one 😊 (+) for ours or 🙁 (-) for theirs; one not rated is put down to whoever attacks the end the ball got to (a hollow dot), once which way we attack is sure. The same colours are the thin strip on the video timeline and the label on the field map
  • Rough: worked out from the nearest players to the ball, so teams need their kits right (👕), and a ball the tracking loses for a while is not known. About 75–80% right against your labelled windows
Events
  • Shift+E (or ✍️ label events) label events, until Esc: pause where it happens, point at the player, and press T touch, P pass or kick, S shot, V save, G goal (when it crosses the line, on the scorer), W won the ball (the touch that won it). A pass, shot or save is a touch too, so label it once with its own key. P is any kick that sends the ball on: a pass, whether it reaches a teammate or not, or a clear / kick up the field with no one in mind. Don't judge it: the next touch (T on whoever gets it, ours or theirs) says how it turned out. Not pointing at anyone: the event has no player. U takes back the last one. While it's on these keys label instead of their usual jobs; space, arrows and , . still move the video
  • Full windows (Events box): a stretch where you label every touch, start to end; a few minutes at a time. They measure what the event finding finds and misses. Labels elsewhere only check what they cover
  • Labels show on the video as a letter on the player near their moment, and as ticks on the timeline (bands = full windows)
Players
  • V show tracked players: boxes by kit (white = our team, blue = opponents, grey dashed = refs, keepers, spectators); orange ring = the player picked next to 👥 (or follow nobody: just the boxes); a small white #number = anyone else already labelled. Which teams get boxes: 🔍 layers (our team only to start) · Shift+D opponents + others on / off
  • L then click a player = that's them (labels the whole tracklet: a stretch the tracker kept on one person)
  • Shift+L = the ring is on the wrong person from here · U take back the last one
  • Paused, point at someone who isn't on our team (a spectator, a parent, a coach, an opponent) and press Shift+X (or 🚫 in the box a click opens): their stretch of the tracklet turns grey and is never labelled or guessed, and a label on it goes. U takes it back
  • Goalies: found ones show GK on their box (green ours, violet theirs; the same colours on the field map). Where one isn't found, point at them and press Shift+G for our goalie, or click them and pick 🧤 our / their goalie: that tracklet is the goalie, and so is anyone in the same kit staying around the goal that team defends. A goalie in their own kit may only have a box with Shift+D (other kits) on. Someone wrongly shown as a goalie: click them and pick ✕ not a goalie (that tracklet is never one, and the deepest of the rest is). How often each is found, and your marks, are in the Game time box (✕ there removes a mark)
  • Kits: a player in a grey box (neither team) who's on a team: click them and pick 👕 ours or 👕 theirs. Their kit colour (as it looks in that light) is that team's for the whole game, so everyone else in it changes too; 👕 neither puts a kit back with the refs and spectators. Who has the ball, and the goalies, go by these teams. Your kit marks are in the Game time box (✕ takes one back). Shift+V (or next grey kit there) jumps to a player in a grey kit nobody's named yet, the most on the field first, outlined in yellow: click them and pick 👕; then it goes on to the next kit. Shift+V again shows another player in the same kit (a kit may mix players with spectators: name it by the players on the field)
  • The orange strip under the timeline = where they're labelled
  • W only them: plays just the stretches where the player you follow is on screen (labelled or guessed), skipping the rest (darkened on the timeline) · W again plays everything. The Player view page does the same across every game
  • Paused, point at a player (cyan box): Y / X answer for them: on a #5? guess, yes it's #5 / no it isn't; on a labelled #5, X = not #5 from here on; on anyone else, Y = whoever they look most like (the hint under the box says who). Click them to pick any number ("looks like" = the closest three; best guess outlined: click it, or Enter; or type a number then Enter)
  • From the Games page's longest unknown tracklets: the game opens there with that tracklet outlined in yellow ("who is this?"); click it to name them. The outline goes once it's labelled
  • S next unknown tracklet (Shift+S back): the same yellow outline, on this game's tracklets of our team nobody's named, longest first (2 s or more). Name them (click), or Shift+X if not our team, and it goes straight on to the next; S again skips one
  • A next automatic guess, any player (Shift+A back): jumps to a #n? and asks (magenta box) · Y yes · X no, and on to the next · A again skips it
  • G next suggestion: jumps to an unlabelled teammate who looks like the player you follow (magenta box, best match first) · Y yes, that's them (labels the tracklet) · X no, and on to the next · G again skips it. Works for any player with a few labels; each answer makes the next suggestions better
  • Dashed orange ring with #38? = where the player you follow probably is, by appearance (not yet labelled; the faint strip above the timeline's orange one) · paused on one, Y confirms it, X says no. A faint #5? = another player placed the same way
